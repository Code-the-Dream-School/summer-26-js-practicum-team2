# Finance Literacy Example Review Notes

These additional review notes apply only to the optional finance-literacy example curriculum under
`shared/content/examples/finance-literacy`. They do not define requirements for other content
packages.

- Present lessons as general education, not individualized financial, legal, tax, credit, or
  investment advice.
- Have a qualified subject-matter reviewer check guidance about regulated or jurisdiction-dependent
  topics.
- Label example budgets and calculations as illustrative, and verify their arithmetic and stated
  assumptions.
- Prefer current primary sources from relevant public agencies and record review dates.
- Keep any required disclaimer visible alongside the affected material.
