# Privacy Policy

\[App Name\] is a financial-literacy learning application operated by **Counting Cents & Making Sense** (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The application provides educational lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, and other features intended to help users learn about budgeting, saving, credit, debt, and financial stability.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to users and parents or legal guardians.

## 1\. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When a user creates an account, we may collect:

- Email address
- Display name or username
- Password or authentication credentials
- Age or age range
- Learning goals and experience level
- Other profile information voluntarily provided by the user

Passwords should be stored using secure authentication practices and should not be stored as readable text.

### Learning and Progress Information

We may collect information about how users interact with the application, including:

- Lessons started or completed
- Quiz answers and scores
- Learning progress
- Points or experience earned
- Streaks, badges, and achievements
- Selected learning goals
- Recommended or unlocked lessons

### Limited Technical Information

Our application and hosting providers may automatically receive limited technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as:

- Internet Protocol address
- Browser or device type
- Operating system
- Date and time of access
- Error logs and security-related activity

We do not use this information for targeted advertising.

## 2\. Financial Information

\[App Name\] is an educational application. We do not ask users to provide bank account numbers, credit-card numbers, income information, Social Security numbers, investment-account information, or other personal financial records.

Users should not enter real financial-account information into lesson answers, quizzes, usernames, profile fields, or other parts of the application.

## 3\. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Save lesson and quiz progress
- Personalize the user’s learning path
- Award points, badges, streaks, and achievements
- Recommend lessons or activities
- Provide quiz results and educational feedback
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application
- Protect the security and integrity of the application
- Respond to user or parent questions
- Meet legal and regulatory requirements

We do not use personal information to provide individualized financial, investment, tax, credit, or legal advice.

## 4\. Children’s Privacy

\[App Name\] is designed to be available to users of different ages, including children.

Before collecting personal information from a child under 13, we will request the consent of the child’s parent or legal guardian when required by applicable law.

A parent or guardian may contact us to:

- Review the personal information collected from their child
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of the child’s account or information
- Withdraw previously provided consent
- Refuse further collection or use of the child’s information

We will not require a child to provide more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in the application’s educational activities.

If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate parental consent, we will delete or restrict that information as required.

Parents and guardians may submit requests using the contact information at the end of this policy.
