## T-1.1.2 — Short FAQ

_Each answer ≤ 3 sentences. Ordered by expected objection weight at signup._

### 1. Is it free?

Yes — every lesson, quiz, and badge is free. There's no paid tier and no ads. We built this so anyone starting out can learn money basics without a paywall.

### 2. How long does it take?

Most lessons take three to five minutes, and a full topic runs about a week at one lesson a day. You can go faster or slower — your progress saves automatically.

### 3. Who is it for?

It's designed for first-time earners: students opening their first checking account and recent grads getting their first paycheck. No prior finance knowledge is assumed.

### 4. Do I need an account?

Yes, so we can save your progress, streaks, and badges across devices. Sign-up takes under a minute with email or Google. You can delete your account anytime from settings.

### 5. Is my data safe?

We never ask for or connect to your real bank accounts. We store only what's needed to run your lessons — like your email and progress — and we don't sell your data to third parties.
